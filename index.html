I am Chenyu Wang, a third-year PhD Candidate in ECE at Boston University, co-advised by Prof. Wenchao Li and Prof. Kayhan Batmanghelich. I am now focused on vision-language models, uncertainty quantification in natural language generation, AI explainability and reasoning, and their applications in the medical domain.

Prior to joining Boston University, I earned a Master’s degree in ECE from the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, and a Bachelor’s degree from Hangzhou Dianzi University.
